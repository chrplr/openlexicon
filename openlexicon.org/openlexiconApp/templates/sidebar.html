Quick how-to
  • Choose a language to see the available datasets in the language below.
  • Click on desired tags. For instance, if you want datasets that give measures of subjective frequency, click on the Subjective frequency tag. Note: a dataset may have several tags (e.g., AoA_FreqSub_1493 gives measures of Age of acquisition and Subjective frequency).
  • Select desired datasets. If you want more informations about a specific dataset, you can hover over its tooltip.
  • Select columns to display for each dataset.
  • For each column in the table, you can:
    • Hover the mouse over the column name to see a brief description
    • Filter using intervals (e.g. 40...500) or regular expressions
    • Sort, ascending or descending
  • Click on the button Show Word List Search to enter words that you want to search in the table.
  • Download the result of your manipulations by clicking on the TSV or EXCEL buttons above the table
