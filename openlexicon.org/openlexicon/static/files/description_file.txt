Auteurs	New, B., Pallier, C., Schalchli, G., Bourgin, J., Gimenes, M.
Site web	http://www.lexique.org
Langue	French
Favori	True
Nb Words	189864
Ref	New, B., Pallier, C., Schalchli, G., Bourgin, J., & Gimenes, M. Lexique 4: a major upgrade of the Lexique French Lexical Database.

Champs
Mot	Orthographic form of the word
Phono	Phonological transcription (Lexique coding system)
Phono_IPA	Phonological transcription in International Phonetic Alphabet
Lemme	Lemma or base form of the word
CGram	Main grammatical category (noun, verb, adjective, etc.)
CGramOrtho	All grammatical categories associated with the orthographic form
Genre	Grammatical gender (m = masculine, f = feminine, e = epicene)
Nombre	Grammatical number (s = singular, p = plural, i = invariant)
InfoVER	Verb inflection information (tense, mood, person)
FreqMot	Frequency of the word form per million words (subtitle corpus)
FreqOrtho	Summed frequency per million of all POS sharing the same orthographic form
FreqLemme	Summed frequency per million of all forms sharing the same lemma
CDOrtho	Contextual diversity of the orthographic form (proportion of documents in which it appears)
IsLem	Indicates whether the form is a lemma (1 = yes, 0 = no)
NbLettres	Number of letters in the word
NbPhons	Number of phonemes
CVOrtho	Orthographic consonant/vowel structure (C/V)
CVPhono	Phonological consonant/vowel structure (C/V/Y)
OLD20	Mean orthographic Levenshtein distance to the 20 nearest neighbors
PLD20	Mean phonological Levenshtein distance to the 20 nearest neighbors
VoisOrtho	Number of orthographic neighbors (Coltheart’s N)
VoisPhono	Number of phonological neighbors
NbHomog	Number of homographs
NbHomoph	Number of homophones
SyllPhono	Phonological syllable segmentation
SyllNb	Number of syllables
SyllCV	Syllabic phonological structure
PUOrtho	Orthographic uniqueness point
PUPhon	Phonological uniqueness point
MorphoBase	Base morpheme of the lemma
MorphoStruct	Morphological structure (prefix-root-suffix counts, e.g., 1-1-1)
MorphoDecomp	Morpheme decomposition with boundary markers
Preval	Word prevalence (percentage of participants knowing the word)
PrevalNb	Number of participants used to compute prevalence
RT_FLP	Mean lexical decision reaction time (French Lexicon Project)
zRT_FLP	Standardized lexical decision reaction time
Err_FLP	Error rate in lexical decision task
