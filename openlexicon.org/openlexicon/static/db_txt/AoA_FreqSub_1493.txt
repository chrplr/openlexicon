Tags	Age of Acquisition, Subjective Frequency
Description	Age of acquisition (AoA) and subjective frequency ratings were collected for the 1,493 most familiar monosyllabic French words among French students. AoA ratings were collected by asking participants to estimate the age in years at which they learned each word. Subjective frequency ratings were collected on a 7-point scale, ranging from never encountered to encountered several times a day.
Auteurs	Ferrand, L., Bonin, P., Méot, A., Augustinova, M., New, B., Pallier, C., & Brysbaert, M.
