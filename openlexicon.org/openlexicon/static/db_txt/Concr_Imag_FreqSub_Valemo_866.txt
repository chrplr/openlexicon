Tags	Concreteness, Subjective Frequency, Emotional Valence
Description	This database presents subjective frequency norms for 660 words in the French language collected from young adults (M = 22.6 years) and older adults (M = 71.2 years). Subjective frequency was assessed using a 7-point scale ranging from “never encountered” to “encountered several times a day”.
