Nom	FreqSub_Imag_ParAge_1286
Tags	Subjective Frequency, Imageability
Description	These standards aim to provide imageability and subjective frequency ratings for four adult age groups ranging from 18 to 85 years old (18-25; 26-39; 40-59; and 60 and older) for 1,286 neutral and emotional French words available in the EMA database (Gobin et al., 2017). Overall, older adults rated words as more frequent (subjectively) and more imageable than younger adults.
