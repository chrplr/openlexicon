Nom	Manulex-Ortho
Tags	General
Description	Manulex is a lexical database that provides word frequencies calculated from a corpus of 54 textbooks (1.9 million words). Word frequency lists are provided for three levels of reading proficiency: CP (6 years old), where children build their vocabulary through phonological mediation; CE1 (7 years old), where they build their orthographic vocabulary through the gradual automation of written word recognition; and cycle 3 (CE2-CM2, 8-11 years old) where the lexical stock is consolidated and enriched through repeated exposure to writing. A fourth level consists of all textbooks from CP to CM2, thus representing the writing addressed to children in primary school.
