Tags	Imageability, Age of Acquisition
Description	This database provides French standards for 400 line drawings taken from Cycowiez, Friedman, Rothstein, and Snodgrass (1997), including the 260 line drawings standardized by Snodgrass and Vanderwart (1980). The images were standardized based on the following variables: name agreement, image agreement, familiarity, visual complexity, image variability, and age of acquisition. These normative data also include word frequency values and the first verbal associate (extracted from Ferrand & Alario, 1998). The six variables obtained are important because of their potential impact in many areas of psychology, particularly the study of cognitive processes such as visual perception, language, and memory.
