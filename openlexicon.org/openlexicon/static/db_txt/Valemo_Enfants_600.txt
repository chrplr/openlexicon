Nom	Valemo_Enfants_600
Tags	Emotional Valence
Description	This study provides a French database for children containing a large corpus of words (N = 600) that have been evaluated for their emotional valence (positive, neutral, and negative) according to the children's age (5, 7, and 9 years) and gender (girls and boys).
Auteurs	Syssau, A., & Monnier, C.
