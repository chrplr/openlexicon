Nom	Voisins
Tags	Orthographic Neighbors
Description	One possible definition of the orthographic neighbors of a word is the words that can be created by changing one letter without changing the position of the other letters (forming a ‘minimal pair’). The `Neighbors` table was constructed from the 130,000 entries in the `Graphemes` database of Lexique262. It consists of the following fields: *Graph: All orthographic entries in Lexique2; *NbVoisOrth: The number of orthographic neighbors; *VoisOrth: The different orthographic neighbors. *FreqVoisOrth: The different frequencies (from the Frantext book database and per million) of each of the neighbors; *FreqCum: The cumulative frequency of all neighbors.
